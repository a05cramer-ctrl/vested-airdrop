How it works

1

Trades make fees

Every $VESTED trade pays pump.fun's creator fee into the coin's creator vault, on the bonding curve and on PumpSwap after migration.

2

:00 claim

At the top of every hour (UTC) the engine claims the vault from the creator wallet and pays out the same minute.

3

Weigh holders

Hold at least 100,000 $VESTED. Your weight is your balance × your multiplier: 1 + the SOL you paid pump.fun in fees over 30 days, capped at 10×.

4

SOL lands

The top 200 wallets by weight split the pot pro-rata. Cuts under 0.001 SOL roll into the next hour.

Rules

  • Every hour at :00 UTC the engine claims $VESTED's accrued pump.fun creator fees (bonding curve creator vault, plus the PumpSwap creator fee after migration) and pays them out the same minute.
  • Eligible: hold at least 100,000 $VESTED (of 1,000,000,000) at the snapshot. The bonding curve, the pool and the creator wallet are never paid.
  • Weight = balance × multiplier. Multiplier = 1 + min(9, F), where F is the SOL your wallet paid pump.fun in fees over the last 30 days.
  • F is estimated at a 1% fee on your pump.fun volume, bonding curves and PumpSwap, read from Helius parsed transactions. 0 fees = 1×, 2 SOL = 3×, 9 SOL or more = 10×.
  • Top 200 eligible holders by weight are paid, pro-rata to weight.
  • Dust: payouts below 0.001 SOL are skipped and roll into the next hour's pot.
  • Receipts: every payout tx carries the memo VESTED|<unix hour>|<total lamports>.
  • Refresh: each wallet's F is looked up at most once every 6 hours.
